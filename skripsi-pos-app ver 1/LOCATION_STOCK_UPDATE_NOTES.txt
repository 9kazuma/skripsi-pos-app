Location/cabang stock update
============================

New behavior:
- Stock is now stored per location/cabang.
- Product page has a Location/Cabang selector.
- Same product can show different stock in Alam Sutera and Kemang.
- Restock and Stock Adjustment now update stock for the selected location only.
- Dashboard stock distribution and stock alerts follow the selected location.
- POS also has a location selector and reduces stock from that selected location.

Important SQL migration:
Before testing, run this in phpMyAdmin:

docs/location_stock_migration.sql

Default locations added:
- Alam Sutera
- Kemang

If you want to rename/add locations, edit table `locations` in phpMyAdmin.
