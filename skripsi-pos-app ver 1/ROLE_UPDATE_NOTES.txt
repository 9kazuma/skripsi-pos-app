Role update implemented:

Roles:
- owner: can access everything, including Reports and Sales Target.
- manager: can access Dashboard, Products, Users, Restock, Adjustment, Profile. Reports are hidden and blocked by backend.
- cashier: can access POS, Sales History, Profile.

User management rules:
- owner can create/edit/delete owner, manager, and cashier users.
- manager can create/edit/delete manager and cashier users.
- manager cannot create owner accounts.
- manager cannot edit/delete owner accounts or change any user role to owner.

Important migration:
Run docs/role_migration_owner_manager_cashier.sql in phpMyAdmin before testing if your database still has old admin/cashier enum roles.
